# Training to Results

Working name for Charles Warloe's training and performance consulting venture.

Help a business with about **50 employees** solve **one costly job-performance problem**. Work with its subject matter expert to define success, diagnose the obstacle, and implement focused training, real-work practice, job aids, and supervisor support. Measure the result the business needs. The final business name remains open.

## Business hypothesis

- **Buyer:** an owner or operations leader at a company with about 50 employees, one HR generalist, and perhaps a recruiter or designated trainer, with limited training capacity.
- **Buying trigger:** a costly, recurring performance failure that existing training has not resolved.
- **Initial offer:** a focused diagnostic of one role and one performance problem, proposed at **$1,450**, followed by separately scoped implementation.
- **Outcomes to measure:** the selected task result and its relationship to business value, with a baseline and follow-up. Possible measures include candidate sourcing, rework, customer handoffs, supervisor time, or independent performance.
- **Client contribution:** domain expertise, real work examples, expert validation, supervisor participation, and access to the chosen result.
- **Charles's contribution:** diagnosis, extracting expert decisions, training design, practice, performance aids, assessment, and follow-up measurement. AI may assist capture and drafting; the client expert validates accuracy.

The new customer, price, and purchase trigger remain hypotheses. Charles reports that all six participants in his initial recruiting pilot achieved five additional qualified candidates for a live requisition in a one-hour assessment, following a six-hour program. He also reports collaborating with Doxa's two founders to codify work and build training and competency checks. Those are historical experience cases, not independent validation of this new fixed-price service. The Doxa work was as an employee; later acquisition does not establish causality. Story lessons have only been tried by Charles.

The 6Ds Company is a methodological reference and possible source of professional development or referrals. Its 2026 workshop tuition is $1,695 per person, not a custom consulting-project quote. No affiliation or formal partnership is claimed. See the case document for source links and details.
