# Practical Employee Development

Helping growing businesses without a training department develop employees who can perform important work independently.

This repository contains Charles Warloe's early-stage learning and development consulting business materials. The current focus is a service for an owner or operations leader: diagnose one role and one performance problem, then implement practical job aids, realistic practice, and supervisor follow-up.

## Business hypothesis

- **Buyer:** an owner or operations leader at a growing small or medium business with recurring employee roles, limited HR support, and no dedicated training department.
- **First problem to test:** repeated supervisor coaching during onboarding and slow progress toward independent work.
- **Initial offer:** a focused diagnostic of one role and one performance problem, proposed at **$1,450**, followed by separately scoped implementation.
- **Outcomes to measure:** supervisor coaching hours, time to independent work, or rework, selected with the customer.
- **Delivery tools:** task standards, performance aids, worked examples, guided and independent practice, interactive stories where useful, and existing customer software.

The customer, price, and purchase trigger remain hypotheses. Prior Doxa employee work supports delivery experience. The later Skis proposal was not implemented. Story lessons have only been tried by Charles. The package distinguishes capability, prior experience, and unvalidated demand.
